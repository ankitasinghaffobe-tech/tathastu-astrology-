✦ Our Story

Born Under Auspicious Stars

Tathastu Astrology was founded in 2018 in New Delhi by Pandit Rajan Sharma, a third-generation Vedic astrologer with over 30 years of practice. Frustrated by the inaccessibility of authentic astrological guidance, he envisioned a platform where every seeker could connect with verified, trustworthy experts at any hour.

The name "Tathastu" — meaning "so be it" in Sanskrit — reflects our commitment to helping you manifest your highest potential through cosmic alignment and personalised wisdom.

Today, we serve over 50,000 satisfied clients monthly across 20 countries, with a network of 120+ screened astrologers across Vedic, KP, Nadi, and Western traditions.

Our Mission

To make genuine Vedic wisdom accessible to every soul, bridging the gap between ancient cosmic knowledge and modern life challenges through technology and trust.
